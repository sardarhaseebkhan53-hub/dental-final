About Junaid Dental Care

Where Families Across Ali Pur Trust Their Smiles

Since 2006, Junaid Dental Care has been the trusted family dental clinic for the Ali Pur and Lehtrar Road community — with more than 16 years of experience. We combine qualified dental specialists, modern digital equipment, and a calm, welcoming atmosphere to deliver dental care that truly makes a difference.

Our Technology

Modern Technology for Safer, Faster, More Comfortable Care

We've invested in the latest dental technology so you benefit from more accurate diagnoses, shorter appointments, and virtually painless treatments.

Digital X-Rays

80% less radiation than traditional X-rays with instant high-resolution images for accurate diagnosis.

3D Imaging & CBCT

Three-dimensional scans for precise implant planning, root canal treatment, and complex cases.

Rotary Endodontics

Laser Dentistry
